Decision description
====================

Facts/Problem
-------------

- The Community Expansion Committee (CEC) started to engage with countries mostly in the South and East (developing countries).
- The general concept of offering a standardized approach for governmental websites is successful - not only in developing countries.
- On several occasions, contacts with leads in countries in Europe have been created.
- The Community Expansion Committee is currently limited to developing countries.
- We should avoid multiple parties going to the same institution/country without knowing it. So exchange of information between the Committee, the Board and the TYPO3 GmbH (including the project ambassador) needs to happen.
- The CEC is collecting information about countries and their potential for TYPO3 in the public sector in a structured way; the idea is to use it also for other countries.

Objective
---------

The CEC wants to create more leads for the standardized approach for governmental websites within Europe.

Obligations to cooperate
------------------------

TYPO3 GmbH / TYPO3 Association Board - to avoid duplicate efforts.

Financial and human resources impacts
-------------------------------------

- None on short notice.
- In the medium term, the team needs to grow to spread the tasks over several shoulders. More activities can lead to the need to increase the funds in later years.

Resolution/Petitum
------------------

Allow the Community Expansion Committee to approach countries within Europe as well, and to be a go-to place to inform if somebody was approached by a country/public institution, so information is tracked in one place (right now, even if some country is using TYPO3, we often don't know it, which makes communication hard).
