Decision description
====================

Facts/Problem
-------------

**Proposed benefit:**

- We will monitor European public tenders where TYPO3 or similar products are mentioned via an external partner (`AIVI Consulting <https://www.aivi-consulting.de/>`__).
- We will receive "raw data", which will be sent as an email newsletter to members.
- We can set up a TYPO3-branded Google Doc where the data will be entered.
- AIVI Consulting will be mentioned as consulting partner, e.g. in the footer of the information, so they could benefit from leads of TYPO3 agencies.
- No costs involved / quid pro quo.
- We will send the news once a week.
- We will send the public tender information to Gold & Platinum members.

**Proposed memberships:**

- Agency: Gold, Platinum

If the TYPO3 GmbH wants, we can also send this information to Solution Partner agencies.

Objective
---------

- Making our memberships more interesting.
- Create a new subscription benefit by sending TYPO3-related calls for tender to members free of charge via a newsletter.
- Agencies no longer need to actively monitor various platforms.
- Agencies not doing public tenders could start doing them (maybe with assistance of AIVI Consulting first).

Obligations to cooperate
------------------------

- AIVI Consulting (external partner)
- Mathias Bolt Lesniak needs to copy & paste the information.
- We might need to set up a better newsletter member list first, addressing the different membership levels.

Financial and human resources impacts
-------------------------------------

- No money, as we would do a "quid pro quo" agreement.
- Mathias Bolt Lesniak already agreed to copy & paste the information to a new newsletter.

Resolution/Petitum
------------------

To make our memberships more attractive, the decision owner would like to introduce real benefits for different membership levels.

- Shall we set up a "quid pro quo" partner contract with AIVI Consulting?
- 1 year trial period, ok?
- At which level to start? Proposal: Gold + Platinum

Discarded alternatives
----------------------
