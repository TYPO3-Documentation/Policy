Decision description
====================

Facts/Problem
-------------

- The teams are getting older, the community is getting older on average.
- TYPO3 has the reputation of being an "old" CMS.
- TYPO3 has the reputation of being "uncool".
- Younger people have fewer connections to the community, old-timers often spend their time with each other.

Objective
---------

- Improve TYPO3's reputation as a cooler, fresh community and system.
- Create connections and pathways for the next generation into the community.
- Connect younger people in their age group through TYPO3.
- Educate younger people on "the ways of the community" - how to contribute, where to go, whom to ask etc.
- Long-term: get younger people into the community teams.

Obligations to cooperate
------------------------

- Mentors are needed at the event to guide the teams along (ideally from the Core Team or other core community teams).
- Marketing and social media teams should be present at the event.
- TYPO3 GmbH should be involved in the organization of the event.

Financial and human resources impacts
-------------------------------------

- Last year's expenses were roughly 55,000 EUR; for this year we aim at a 60,000 EUR budget, with leeway for adding hardship tickets. The calculation is based on the cost for the event location (~35,000 EUR), flights for the mentors, social activities (like team events) at the location and soft drinks.
- Time-wise, the mentors will need to be at the event and have some time (~5 days of work) before and after the event to get and keep in touch with the participants.
- The social media and marketing team will need additional time after the event to create posts and videos.

Resolution/Petitum
------------------

Is the Board willing to provide a budget of 60,000 EUR for the Surfcamp 2025? The budget will be split over two years.
