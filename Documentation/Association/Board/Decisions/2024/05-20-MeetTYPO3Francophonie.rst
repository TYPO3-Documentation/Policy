Decision description
====================

Facts/Problem
-------------

- Visiting *Le Rendez-vous d'affaires de la Francophonie* will support the Community Expansion Committee's efforts to promote TYPO3 in francophone Africa and, of course, Canada.
- Neither of the Community Expansion Committee co-leaders will be able to attend this event.

Objective
---------

- Permission for Philippe Fekete to represent TYPO3 on behalf of the Community Expansion Committee at an upcoming conference for francophone business in Quebec.
- This means the event can be listed on typo3.org and Philippe can represent the TYPO3 Association in accordance with the `Meet TYPO3 Requirements <https://docs.typo3.org/m/typo3/guide-contentandmarketing/main/en-us/Events/MeetTypo3.html>`__.

Obligations to cooperate
------------------------

- Philippe Fekete
- TYPO3 Community Expansion Committee

Financial and human resources impacts
-------------------------------------

Better visibility and impact created from resources already spent. (The Community Expansion Committee is already covering half of the participation cost for the event.)

Resolution/Petitum
------------------

Permission for Philippe Fekete to represent TYPO3 on behalf of the Community Expansion Committee at an upcoming conference for francophone business in Quebec.

Discarded alternatives
----------------------

None
