Decision description
====================

Facts/Problem
-------------

**General information:** To make our memberships more attractive, the decision owner would like to introduce real benefits for different membership levels.

**Proposed benefit:**

- We will explicitly allow only members of a certain tier upwards to use the TYPO3 brand in online ads, such as Google Ads or any kind of social media ads.

**Proposed memberships:**

- Agency: Silver, Gold, Platinum
- Academic: Silver, Gold (but only for jobs)

**Additional:**

- The **TYPO3 GmbH** also wants to have this for **Solution Partners** (and would cover their membership fees in case someone doesn't have one).
- **We could** start collecting trademark infringements from others (via a form on our website with the most common search engines, social media platforms and screenshots) at some point. This would ease the work for Ric van Westreenen.

Objective
---------

- Making our memberships more interesting.
- Free rider agencies will be forced to sign up for a membership of at least Silver level.
- According to Ric van Westreenen, this would strengthen our trademark.

Obligations to cooperate
------------------------

- Ric van Westreenen, for monitoring the ad usages manually in the beginning.
- Later on Google (and presumably others as well), which automatically refuse ads with trademark usage of "TYPO3".
- Low tier memberships and "free riders".

Financial and human resources impacts
-------------------------------------

- Maybe slightly more reimbursement money for Ric van Westreenen, as he needs to file the complaints with Google, etc. first.
- Ric van Westreenen already agreed to perform the tasks.

Resolution/Petitum
------------------

- Shall we allow the usage of our "TYPO3" trademark only for Agency members, starting at Silver level and above?
- Shall we allow the usage of our "TYPO3" trademark only for Academic members, starting at Silver level and above (for job advertisements)?
- Shall we allow the usage of our "TYPO3" trademark also for TYPO3 GmbH Solution Partners (given that the GmbH covers the Silver membership in case they are no member)?

Discarded alternatives
----------------------

None
