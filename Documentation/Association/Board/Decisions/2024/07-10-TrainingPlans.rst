Decision description
====================

Facts/Problem
-------------

**General information:** To make our memberships more attractive, the decision owner would like to introduce real benefits for different membership levels.

**Proposed benefit:**

- We will offer premade "apprenticeship / training plans" (German "Ausbildungsrahmenplan") to download for specific membership levels.
- These official training plans can be used by agencies or academic members to educate their apprentices towards becoming TCCI and TCCD, next to the mandatory knowledge e.g. for "Fachinformatiker Anwendungsentwicklung" ("IT specialist for application development").

**Proposed memberships:**

- Agency: Silver, Gold, Platinum
- Academic: Silver (to be discussed), Gold

Objective
---------

- Making our memberships more interesting.
- Increase in memberships.

Obligations to cooperate
------------------------

- SkillDisplay will facilitate and organize the sprints (see separate offer).
- The Education Committee will participate in these sprints (reimbursements expected).

Financial and human resources impacts
-------------------------------------

- Education Committee: reimbursements of approx. 2,000 - 4,000 EUR, depending on how many people will attend.
- SkillDisplay: 10,760 EUR

Resolution/Petitum
------------------

Shall we provide official, downloadable "apprenticeship / training plans" for members?

Discarded alternatives
----------------------

None
