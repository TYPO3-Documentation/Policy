=========================================
Voting Procedure of the TYPO3 Association
=========================================

:Document Owner: TYPO3 Association Board
:Version: 3.0, 10.11.2026

..  _voting-procedure-1:

1. Scope, Composition and Term of Office
========================================

..  _voting-procedure-art-1:

Art. 1 Scope
------------

#.  This Voting Procedure governs the election of the Board and of the Business Control Committee (BCC) of the TYPO3 Association.
#.  It applies to ordinary and extraordinary elections. The :ref:`Bylaws of the TYPO3 Association <bylaws-de>` take precedence over this Voting Procedure.

..  _voting-procedure-art-2:

Art. 2 Composition of the Board
-------------------------------

#.  The Board shall consist of 7 to 8 members.
#.  If the number of Board members falls below the minimum under :ref:`Art. 18 of the Bylaws <bylaws-de-18>`, :ref:`Art. 19 of the Bylaws <bylaws-de-19>` applies.
#.  The Board consists of the President, the Vice President, the Secretary, the Treasurer and the further members. It determines these functions by simple majority of the Board members present at the annual meeting of the Board before the start of the new term of office.
#.  The candidate for the presidency should have been a member of the Board for at least one year. Otherwise, the first term in the presidency is limited to one year.

..  _voting-procedure-art-3:

Art. 3 Term of Office and Staggered Elections
---------------------------------------------

#.  The term of office of a Board member is three years. It ends with the ordinary General Assembly of the third year of office (:ref:`Art. 19 of the Bylaws <bylaws-de-19>`: Election Procedure and Term of Office).
#.  The seats are filled on a staggered basis: each year, the seats whose term of office ends are up for election. In by-elections, the by-election seats are allocated to the remaining candidates who received the most votes. In by-elections, the Board decides by resolution before the election on the number of seats and the term of office.

..  _voting-procedure-art-4:

Art. 4 Business Control Committee (BCC)
---------------------------------------

#.  :ref:`Arts. 9 to 12 <voting-procedure-4>` apply accordingly to the election of the BCC members.
#.  If the number of elected BCC members falls below the minimum size set out in the Bylaws, by-elections are announced within 4 weeks.

2. Timetable and Deadlines
==========================

..  _voting-procedure-art-5:

Art. 5 Timetable
----------------

#.  The timetable is based on the day of the General Assembly (Day 0). All times are given in CET/CEST.

3. Announcement, Nomination, Discussion and Eligibility to Vote
===============================================================

..  _voting-procedure-art-6:

Art. 6 Announcement
-------------------

#.  The Voting Committee announces the election via the members mailing list and a news post on typo3.org. The second announcement reminds the members via the members mailing list.
#.  The announcement states: the number of seats to be filled and their term of office (for vacancies, the remaining term), whether a substitute is elected, the dates of the three possible voting rounds and the date of the General Assembly.
#.  It reminds all members to check their membership profile and email address, because the voting codes (voting tokens) are sent to the email address stored in the membership profile. Each member must ensure that this address is up to date 2 weeks before the start of the election. A subsequent dispatch to a different email address is not possible.
#.  The election must be put on the agenda in the invitation to the General Assembly.

..  _voting-procedure-art-7:

Art. 7 Nomination
-----------------

#.  Any natural person who is a member of the Association may offer themselves for election or nominate another person, who must confirm their candidacy in advance. The nomination must be received via the nomination form (`https://voting.typo3.org <https://voting.typo3.org>`__) during the nomination phase.
#.  Minimum data: first name, last name, email, picture, the election or office the application is for, and a statement on motivation and goals and on the relationship to the TYPO3 project.
#.  A nomination is possible only for either the Board or the BCC.
#.  A person is eligible for election if they can prove a personal membership (Community or Bronze).
#.  The Board may reject a nomination if the nominee cannot demonstrate a relationship to the TYPO3 project or has harmed the project or the Association.
#.  The exclusions under the :ref:`Conflict of Interest Policy <conflict-interest>` apply to whether a nomination is possible.

..  _voting-procedure-art-8:

Art. 8 Discussion Platform and Eligibility to Vote
--------------------------------------------------

#.  Members can discuss with all nominees on an online platform of the Association. The discussion phase begins 6 weeks before the election.
#.  The Voting Committee invites every member to the online election. The election begins no later than 14 and no earlier than 28 days before the General Assembly, in each case at 0:00.
#.  The electoral roll is drawn up 2 weeks before the start of the first voting round and is valid for the entire duration of the election. Eligible to vote is anyone **who has paid the admission fee and has paid the current annual fee**.

..  _voting-procedure-4:

4. Election Process
===================

..  _voting-procedure-art-9:

Art. 9 Casting of Votes
-----------------------

#.  Every eligible voter has as many votes as there are seats to be filled. Example: if three Board seats are to be filled, the voter has three votes.
#.  A maximum of one vote per candidate is permitted. Accumulating votes or cross-voting is not possible. Fewer votes than seats may be cast; abstentions are permitted and are not taken into account when determining the number of valid votes.

..  _voting-procedure-art-10:

Art. 10 Required Majority
-------------------------

#.  For the purpose of multi-seat elections, the majority of cast votes (*abgegebene Stimmen*) required by :ref:`Bylaws Art. 19 <bylaws-de-19>` is defined and calculated as 50 % of the validly submitted ballots.
#.  In all three voting rounds, a candidate is elected who receives at least 50 % of the valid ballots of the round (:ref:`Bylaws Art. 19 <bylaws-de-19>`). A ballot submitted via the online tool that contains at least one valid candidate vote counts as a valid ballot. Ballots containing only abstentions do not count.
#.  There is no relative majority (:ref:`Art. 11 paras. 3 and 5 <voting-procedure-art-11>`).

..  _voting-procedure-art-11:

Art. 11 Voting Rounds
---------------------

#.  **Round 1:** All admitted nominees stand for election. Candidates who reach the majority are elected in the order of their number of votes, at most as many as there are seats to be filled. If seats remain open after the first Round, a second round will take place.
#.  **Round 2:** Eligible for the second round are the remaining candidates. The number of candidates in the second round is limited to at most twice as many persons as there are seats to be filled. Those candidates in the second round are chosen from those who received the most votes in the previous round but did not reach the majority. In the event of a tie for the last place, all tied candidates stand for election. If there are fewer candidates, all of them stand for election. The majority under :ref:`Art. 10 para. 1 <voting-procedure-art-10>` applies.
#.  **Round 3:** If seats still remain open after Round 2, a third and final round is held following the same pattern (:ref:`Bylaws Art. 19 <bylaws-de-19>`: up to two further rounds).
#.  If only one candidate stands for election in a round, a YES/NO vote is held. The candidate is elected if he or she receives at least 50 % of the YES votes.
#.  Seats that remain open after Round 3 remain vacant. Only if the third round provides a result, in which the minimum number of Board or BCC members is not reached, an Extraordinary General Assembly will take place and the voting is done during that Assembly.
#.  By accepting their nomination, candidates declare that they will accept the election.

..  _voting-procedure-art-12:

Art. 12 Tie and Drawing of Lots
-------------------------------

#.  In the event of a tie for a seat, the drawing of lots decides (:ref:`Bylaws Art. 15 <bylaws-de-15>`).
#.  The Voting Committee draws the lot in the presence of at least two witnesses who are not candidates and records it in the protocol.

..  _voting-procedure-5:

5. Vacancies during the Term of Office
======================================

..  _voting-procedure-art-13:

Art. 13 Leaving the Board
-------------------------

#.  A Board member leaves office early through resignation, death, loss of membership or removal by the General Assembly.
#.  Resignation is declared to the Board in writing, as early as possible. Anyone who does not wish to continue in office until the end of the term of office informs the Board at least 6 months before it ends.

..  _voting-procedure-art-14:

Art. 14 Determination of a Vacancy
----------------------------------

#.  The Board informs the Voting Committee and the members of any vacancy without delay.
#.  The vacated position runs only until the end of the term of office of the departed member (remaining term). The staggering under :ref:`Art. 3 <voting-procedure-art-3>` is retained.
#.  If the remaining term is less than 6 months and the Board still has at least the minimum size, the seat may remain vacant.

..  _voting-procedure-art-15:

Art. 15 Functions within the Board
----------------------------------

#.  If a member holding a function under :ref:`Art. 2 para. 3 <voting-procedure-art-2>` leaves, the Board redetermines the function from among its members. :ref:`Art. 2 para. 4 <voting-procedure-art-2>` applies to the presidency.

..  _voting-procedure-6:

6. Voting Committee, Protocol and Final Provisions
==================================================

..  _voting-procedure-art-16:

Art. 16 Voting Committee
------------------------

#.  The Voting Committee is responsible for the online election. It sets up the platform and checks the validity of the election.
#.  The Board appoints the Voting Committee. It consists of at least three members. Members of the Voting Committee must not take part in a running election as candidates.
#.  After each voting round, the Voting Committee checks the result, announces the candidates for the next round, conducts drawings of lots and supports the Board with the succession (:ref:`Art. 19 of the Bylaws <bylaws-de-19>`).

..  _voting-procedure-art-17:

Art. 17 Protocol
----------------

#.  The Voting Committee keeps a protocol of the election containing: the documentation of the electorate, the timetable of all actions, a protocol of all email errors, the result of each voting round, the substitute and the succession list, and all drawings of lots.

..  _voting-procedure-art-18:

Art. 18 Retention of Election Data
----------------------------------

#.  The data in the online voting tool is kept until the start of the next nomination phase. With its start, all expired elections and polls are removed.
#.  The protocol under :ref:`Art. 17 <voting-procedure-art-17>` is retained permanently.

..  _voting-procedure-art-19:

Art. 19 Contesting Resolutions
------------------------------

#.  Members may contest resolutions of the General Assembly that violate the law or the Bylaws within one month of becoming aware of them (:ref:`Art. 14 of the Bylaws <bylaws-de-14>`).

..  _voting-procedure-art-20:

Art. 20 Entry into Force
------------------------

#.  This Voting Procedure replaces version 2.0 of 13 November 2025. It enters into force upon approval by the Board on [date].

..  _voting-procedure-art-21:

Art. 21 Transitional Provision on Staggered Elections
-----------------------------------------------------

#.  The existing staggering of the terms of office remains unaffected. The number of seats to be filled each year results from the expiring terms of office, at most three (:ref:`Art. 19 of the Bylaws <bylaws-de-19>`).
#.  After a new election of the entire Board (:ref:`Art. 19 of the Bylaws <bylaws-de-19>`), the 3 candidates with the most votes have a term of office of 3 years, the next three receive a term of office of 2 years, and the rest a term of office of 1 year.
