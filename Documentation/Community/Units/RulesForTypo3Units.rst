=====================
Rules for TYPO3 Units
=====================

..  contents:: Table of Contents
    :local:
    :depth: 2

**Note on imperatives:** The keywords must, must not, required, shall,
shall not, should, should not, recommended, may, and optional in this
document are to be interpreted as described in `RFC
2119 <https://www.ietf.org/rfc/rfc2119.txt>`__.

..  _unit-rules-definition:

1 Definition
============

Units are self-managed groups of TYPO3 contributors working together to
deliver value to the TYPO3 Product within a specific responsibility
domain. Under the leadership of a Unit Coordinator and governed by the
TYPO3 Association, the Units aim to be consensus-driven implementers of
the TYPO3 Product Roadmap and TYPO3 Product Strategy (that is itself
created through input from the Units in the Unit Cooperation Panel) and
the TYPO3 Association Strategy.

..  _unit-rules-constitution-charter:

2 Constitution and Charter
==========================

..  _unit-rules-governing-authority:

2.1 Governing Authority
-----------------------

A Unit is created, modified, and terminated as defined in the TYPO3
Association Bylaws. Where nothing else is defined in the TYPO3
Association Bylaws, Units shall be considered equal to committees.

..  _unit-rules-charter:

2.2 Charter
-----------

The Unit must have a charter defined by the TYPO3 Association Board. The
charter must define at least:

1. The Unit’s name
2. Responsibility domain
3. Scope of the Unit’s delegated decision-making authority
4. Names of at least five initial Members

..  _unit-rules-name:

2.3 Name
--------

A Unit’s name must be the word “TYPO3” followed by a noun or a short
list of nouns describing the responsibility domain followed by the word
“Unit”. Unit names can contain commas and the word “and” to separate
nouns in a list. (Examples: *TYPO3 Knowledge Unit* or *TYPO3 Maintenance
and Security Unit*)

..  _unit-rules-delegated-decision-authority:

2.4 Delegated Decision-Making Authority
---------------------------------------

The Charter must define and clearly limit which decision-making powers
are delegated to the Unit, represented by the Unit Coordinator. A Unit
can only be delegated powers for operational decisions.

..  _unit-rules-petitioning:

2.5 Petitioning
---------------

Other TYPO3 Association bodies may petition a Unit. Petitions must be
addressed to the Unit Coordinator.

..  _unit-rules-size:

2.6 Minimum Size
----------------

A Unit must have five Members including Unit Coordinator and Deputy
Coordinator.

..  _unit-rules-non-compliance:

2.7 Non-Compliance
------------------

If a Unit cannot comply with the requirements of its Charter or these
Rules, the TYPO3 Association Board must take appropriate action to
rectify it.

..  _unit-rules-structure:

3 Structure
===========

..  _unit-rules-required-roles:

3.1 Required Roles
------------------

The following roles must be present in all Units.

..  _unit-rules-coordinator:

3.1.1 Coordinator
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

..  _unit-rules-coordinator-a:

a. Tasks
""""""""

The Unit Coordinator reports on, prioritizes, delegates, and monitors
the execution of mandated tasks within the Unit and its compliance with
the Unit’s Charter and other applicable rules set forth by the TYPO3
Association.

..  _unit-rules-coordinator-b:

b. Final Say
""""""""""""

The Unit Coordinator has final say in all matters concerning the Unit’s
mandated tasks and day-to-day operation. They are expected to emphasize
a consensus-driven approach and should involve the Unit Council in all
major decisions.

..  _unit-rules-coordinator-c:

c. Accountability
"""""""""""""""""

The Unit Coordinator is answerable to the TYPO3 Association Board.

..  _unit-rules-deputy-coordinator:

3.1.2 Deputy Coordinator
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

..  _unit-rules-deputy-coordinator-a:

a. Tasks
""""""""

The Deputy Unit Coordinator’s primary task is to support the Unit
Coordinator and execute tasks as delegated by the Unit Coordinator.

..  _unit-rules-deputy-coordinator-b:

b. Acting Unit Coordinator
""""""""""""""""""""""""""

If the Unit Coordinator is indisposed or while the Unit Coordinator
position is unfilled, the Deputy Unit Coordinator serves as the acting
Unit Coordinator, taking over all related tasks and responsibilities.

..  _unit-rules-deputy-coordinator-c:

c. Rights and Obligations
"""""""""""""""""""""""""

Unless stated otherwise, the Deputy Unit Coordinator’s rights and
obligations are equal to those of a Unit Member.

..  _unit-rules-member:

3.1.3 Member
~~~~~~~~~~~~

..  _unit-rules-member-a:

a. Definition
"""""""""""""

Unit Members are individuals with relevant knowledge and expertise that
are permanent and consistent contributors to the fulfillment of tasks
under the Unit’s Charter.

..  _unit-rules-member-b:

b. Accountability
"""""""""""""""""

Though Unit Members should be awarded a large degree of freedom in task
selection, Unit Members answer to the Unit Coordinator and must fulfill
delegated tasks promptly and to the best of their ability.

..  _unit-rules-member-c:

c. Reporting
""""""""""""

Unit Members must keep the Unit Coordinator informed about task status
and must without undue delay report any factor that may affect their
ability to complete a task.

..  _unit-rules-member-d:

d. Recruitment
""""""""""""""

The Unit Coordinator must define a contact point for prospective Unit
Members and respond to enquiries in a timely and welcoming manner.

..  _unit-rules-member-e:

e. Expiry
"""""""""

Unless the Unit Coordinator decides otherwise, a Unit Member role
automatically expires when a person has had no assigned task or no
activity in the Unit for six months.

..  _unit-rules-supporter:

3.1.4 Supporter
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

..  _unit-rules-supporter-a:

a. Definition
"""""""""""""

Unit Supporters are individuals with relevant knowledge and expertise
who intermittently contribute to the fulfillment of tasks under the
Unit’s Charter, as assigned by the Unit Coordinator or their delegate.

..  _unit-rules-supporter-b:

b. Commencement and Expiry
""""""""""""""""""""""""""

A person attains the Unit Supporter role when a task is formally
assigned to them by the Unit Coordinator or their delegate. Unless the
Unit Coordinator decides otherwise, a Unit Supporter role automatically
expires when a person has had no assigned task in the Unit for three
months.

..  _unit-rules-supporter-c:

c. Unfilled Role
""""""""""""""""

The role of Supporter may be temporarily unfilled due to recruitment
issues.

..  _unit-rules-supporter-d:

d. Recruitment
""""""""""""""

The Unit Coordinator must define a contact point for prospective Unit
Supporters and respond to enquiries in a timely and welcoming manner.

A Unit Supporter should fulfill delegated tasks and meet the
expectations of a Unit Member for at least six months before they may
become a Unit Member. The Unit Coordinator decides on new Unit Members
in consultation with the Unit Council.

..  _unit-rules-supporter-e:

e. Accountability
"""""""""""""""""

The Unit Supporter is free to decline assigned tasks at any time, but
should fulfill any accepted tasks to the best of their ability.

..  _unit-rules-supporter-f:

f. Reporting
""""""""""""

The Unit Supporter must keep the Unit Coordinator or their delegate
informed about task status and must without undue delay report any
factor that may affect their ability to complete a task.

..  _unit-rules-council:

3.1.5 Unit Council
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

..  _unit-rules-council-a:

a. Definition
"""""""""""""

The Unit Coordinator, Deputy Unit Coordinator, and Unit Members
constitute the Unit Council.

..  _unit-rules-council-b:

b. Coordinator Eligibility
""""""""""""""""""""""""""

Only the Unit’s own Members are eligible to be elected as Coordinator
and Deputy Coordinator of their Unit.

..  _unit-rules-council-c:

c. Meeting Frequency
""""""""""""""""""""

The Unit Council meets whenever the Unit Coordinator or at least 50% of
all eligible Unit Council members desire it. Meetings should take place
at least once every month. A meeting date and time must be announced at
least seven days in advance, unless 50% of all eligible Unit Council
members agree to disregard this rule.

..  _unit-rules-council-d:

d. Meeting Chair
""""""""""""""""

The Council meeting is chaired by the Unit Coordinator or their
delegate.

..  _unit-rules-council-e:

e. Quorum
"""""""""

The Unit Council reaches a quorum when half of all its members, along
with either the Unit Coordinator or Deputy Unit Coordinator, are
present.

..  _unit-rules-council-f:

f. Majority
"""""""""""

All Unit Council decisions require a simple majority of the votes cast.

..  _unit-rules-council-g:

g. Coordinator Term and Re-Election
"""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

The Unit Council elects the Unit Coordinator and Deputy Unit Coordinator
for a term of one year and may re-elect incumbents indefinitely.
Elections for a scheduled new term in office must take place 1–3 months
before that term is due to begin. If the office is vacant, including
when the Unit is first established, the Unit Council must elect the role
as soon as practicable, and the elected role’s term begins immediately
upon election.

..  _unit-rules-council-h:

h. Coordinator Removal
""""""""""""""""""""""

The Unit Council can petition the TYPO3 Association Board to remove the
Unit Coordinator or Deputy Unit Coordinator. For this petition only, a
quorum may be reached without the Coordinator or Deputy present.

..  _unit-rules-council-i:

i. Coordinator Petitioning and Council Consultation
"""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

The Unit Council can petition the Unit Coordinator on and should be
consulted in decisions concerning at least these areas:

1. Appointment of new Unit Members
2. Removal of Unit Members
3. Long-Term Task Prioritization
4. Budgetary Policy
5. Creation and removal of optional structures (see 3.2)
6. Contribution Rules and Acceptance Criteria
7. Petitions by other TYPO3 Association bodies

..  _unit-rules-council-j:

j. Supporters’ Rights
"""""""""""""""""""""

Unit Supporters have no voting rights, but may attend Unit Council
meetings, unless otherwise decided by the Unit Coordinator, Unit Deputy
Coordinator, or Unit Council.

..  _unit-rules-optional-structure:

3.2 Optional Structure
----------------------

..  _unit-rules-optional-types:

3.2.1 Types
~~~~~~~~~~~

The Unit Coordinator may choose to add the optional internal structures
mentioned in this section to the Unit and delegate tasks to the Optional
Structure’s Coordinator.

*  **Initiative:** A Unit Initiative is a group responsible for
   executing a specific task with a clearly specified deliverable and
   deadline. The group dissolves when the task is completed.
*  **Working Group:** A Unit Working Group is a group responsible for a
   specific topic within the Unit’s responsibility domain. The group
   exists until it is removed by the Unit Coordinator.

..  _unit-rules-optional-roles:

3.2.2 Roles
~~~~~~~~~~~

..  _unit-rules-optional-roles-a:

a. Definition
"""""""""""""

Optional Structures must have roles that correspond to the required Unit
roles (see 1.2.2) and share similar responsibilities within their
respective contexts.

*  Coordinator, answerable to the Unit Coordinator, etc.
*  Deputy Coordinator
*  Members
*  Council
*  Supporters

..  _unit-rules-optional-roles-b:

b. Size
"""""""

Optional Structures must consist of at least two people. Coordinator and
Deputy Coordinator are required roles.

..  _unit-rules-optional-roles-c:

c. Inability to Elect Coordinator
"""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

If an Optional Structure consists of fewer than five people and it is
unable to select Coordinator and Deputy, the Unit Coordinator must
disband the Optional Structure.

..  _unit-rules-optional-roles-d:

d. Role Elegibility
"""""""""""""""""""

Unit Members can be Coordinators, Deputy Coordinators, and Members
within an Optional Structure. Unit Supporters can only hold a Supporter
role within an Optional Structure.

..  _unit-rules-external-affiliation:

3.3 External Affiliation
------------------------

..  _unit-rules-t3a-membership:

3.3.1 Membership in the TYPO3 Association
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

..  _unit-rules-t3a-membership-a:

a. Required Association Membership
""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

The role of Coordinator, Deputy Coordinator, and Member in a Unit
requires active personal memberships in the TYPO3 Association.

..  _unit-rules-t3a-membership-b:

b. Association Membership Expiry
""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

If a person’s membership lapses, the membership must be renewed within
30 days. If the person fails to renew the membership within this time,
they lose their role with immediate effect.

..  _unit-rules-other-membership:

3.3.2 Membership in other Units
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

..  _unit-rules-other-membership-a:

a. Right to Combine
"""""""""""""""""""

Any role in a Unit may be combined with any role in another Unit.

..  _unit-rules-other-membership-b:

b. Duty to Inform
"""""""""""""""""

Any person with such combined roles must declare them in writing to the
affected Unit Coordinators. Unit Coordinators must declare their
combined roles to the TYPO3 Association Board.

..  _unit-rules-transparency-reporting:

4 Transparency and Reporting
============================

..  _unit-rules-documentation:

4.1 Documentation of Decisions, Structures, Authority, and Roles
----------------------------------------------------------------

..  _unit-rules-documentation-a:

a. Type and Platform
""""""""""""""""""""

The Unit Coordinator and their delegates must maintain documentation of
all tasks, decisions, structures, delegated authority, roles, and role
assignments. This information must be made publicly available on a
suitable platform, unless the TYPO3 Association Board makes exceptions
to this rule in the Unit Charter.

..  _unit-rules-documentation-b:

b. Further Guidelines
"""""""""""""""""""""

The TYPO3 Association Board may provide further guidelines and
requirements for the information and publication platform.

..  _unit-rules-reporting:

4.2 Regular Reporting
---------------------

..  _unit-rules-reporting-a:

a. Regularity
"""""""""""""

The Unit Coordinator must regularly publish overview reports on the
Unit’s activities and budget status to the TYPO3 Association Members.

..  _unit-rules-reporting-b:

b. Report Contents
""""""""""""""""""

The report must contain information on active and completed tasks for
the reporting period, as well as a forecast of expected active and
completed tasks for the following period.

..  _unit-rules-reporting-c:

c. Publication Platform
"""""""""""""""""""""""

The report must be published on
`news.typo3.com <http://news.typo3.com>`__ every three months, unless
otherwise stated in the Unit Charter.

..  _unit-rules-criteria:

4.3 Contribution Rules and Acceptance Criteria
----------------------------------------------

..  _unit-rules-criteria-a:

a. Publication and Platform
"""""""""""""""""""""""""""

In consultation with the Unit Council, the Unit Coordinator must publish
rules and objective acceptance criteria for contributions on a suitable
platform, as specified by the TYPO3 Association Board or the Unit
Charter.

..  _unit-rules-criteria-b:

b. Cross-Unit Compatibility
"""""""""""""""""""""""""""

The rules and criteria should to the largest possible extent be the same
or compatible with the rules and criteria of other Units.

..  _unit-rules-criteria-c:

c. Override
"""""""""""

The Unit Coordinator may choose to override the contribution rules and
acceptance criteria in cases where:

1. Rules or criteria are obviously lacking coverage for the topic at
   hand.
2. The contribution does not comply with the Unit’s Charter or other
   applicable rules set forth by the TYPO3 Association.

..  _unit-rules-criteria-d:

d. Missing Coverage Handling
""""""""""""""""""""""""""""

Where coverage is found to be missing, the Unit Coordinator must amend
the Contribution Rules and Acceptance Criteria without undue delay.

..  _unit-rules-faq:

Frequently Asked Questions
==========================

**1. What role is the Coordinator really?**
    It may be good to think of the Coordinator as a project manager,
    like what you find in a web agency. A leader with organizing and
    delegating as their main task.

**2. It looks like a Coordinator carries a lot of responsibility and a lot of tasks. How is this realistically achievable?**
    It is important to note that one of the Coordinator’s main
    responsibilities is to delegate tasks away from themselves. The
    workload will differ from unit to unit, but some kind of
    compensation will be necessary, either as time donated
    individually, from an employer, or as a budget decision by the
    TYPO3 Association Board.

**3. It may be a question of definition exactly what lies within the responsibility of a unit. Who makes decisions in these cases, for example what is defined as a feature or that code should be backported?**
    It is the Unit Cooperation Panel’s job to coordinate between the
    units and resolve matters of jurisdiction or conflicts of interest
    between Units.

**4. I don’t want someone to decide what I should do. Do I have a place in a Unit?**
    Yes, you can be a Unit Supporter. It will be difficult for you to
    be a Unit Member because that level of commitment may require you
    to fulfill tasks you do not want to do, but that are necessary for
    the greater good of the project.

**5. What happens if nobody wants to take on the Unit Coordinator or Deputy Unit Coordinator role?**
    If a unit is without a coordinator because nobody wants to take on
    the leadership, this would be a very serious problem. It is the
    project’s problem and no longer something unit-internal. It will
    have to be escalated to the TYPO3 Association Board and will
    probably require re-chartering or re-establishing the Unit.

**6. What does it mean that the Unit Coordinator is answerable to the TYPO3 Association Board?**
    Though the Unit Coordinator is elected by the Unit Council, their
    responsibility is to the TYPO3 Project, represented by the TYPO3
    Association. Responsibility for the Association’s day-to-day
    operations is delegated to the TYPO3 Association Board by the
    General Assembly. If the Unit Coordinator fails to meet the needs
    and requirements of the Project, the TYPO3 Association Board may
    ultimately remove the Unit Coordinator from the role, and the Unit
    Council will have to elect a new Unit Coordinator.

**7. Does a Unit Coordinator or other person in a Unit role have to be employed by the TYPO3 Association or TYPO3 Company?**
    No. There is no rule either way and they may be employed by anyone
    or no one, depending on what is the best solution for the
    individual and the project.

**8. What are good examples of a suitable platform for publication of decisions, structures, delegated authority, roles, and role assignments?**
    For example, tasks may be made available in a project management
    system, decisions in a Git repository, and structures and roles on
    a web page.

**9. Does the Deputy Unit Coordinator as acting Unit Coordinator also answer to the TYPO3 Association Board?**
    Yes, since the acting Unit Coordinator takes over all tasks and
    responsibilities of the Unit Coordinator, they are answerable to
    the TYPO3 Association Board in the same way as any Unit
    Coordinator.

**10. That the membership role automatically expires feels very absolute. What about people taking a sabbatical, for example?**
    The Unit Coordinator can make exceptions, but it’s good to have a
    general rule with that possibility. The responsibility for
    monitoring expiry is currently with the Member first, which is a
    way to give people clear responsibilities and not overload the
    Coordinator.

**11. What if a task is too hard for someone to complete or problems show up during the work on a task?**
    It is the responsibility of a task delegate to report about task
    status and any factor that may affect their ability to complete a
    task. Once such an issue has been reported, the person responsible
    for delegating the task must try to solve the problem by providing
    help, tasking others with providing the help, or delegating the
    task to others.

**12. What are good rules and objective acceptance criteria?**
    This is an important topic, but it is beyond the scope of these
    Rules to define philosophy. This is a topic for the Unit
    Cooperation Panel. It should be a constant point of improvement
    and a living discussion within the community at large.

**13. What happens if multiple units need to have the same contribution rules and acceptance criteria?**
    The Units already have a requirement to make their rules and
    criteria compatible with other units to the largest possible
    extent and the Unit Cooperation Panel is responsible for
    coordinating and harmonizing rules. In addition, two or more units
    may have a requirement to keep exactly the same rules and criteria
    laid down in their respective Unit Charters.

**14. Why doesn’t these rules define who is allowed to merge code?**
    As not all units deal with code and use “merge” as the verb, these
    rules use “acceptance” as a more generally applicable word. The
    Unit Coordinator is required to publish rules and objective
    acceptance criteria for contributions. Ultimately, the acceptance
    (for code, the merge decision) is up to the Unit Coordinator, but
    this person can also delegate such tasks.

**15. Why do these rules not address occasional contributions that are made without a task assignment?**
    This kind of ad hoc, informal contribution is warmly welcome, but
    it comes from outside of the unit, so the rules don’t require them
    to comply with any organizational requirements. However, the way
    the Unit handles the contribution once it is submitted is covered
    by these rules.

**16. Could the Unit Charter define specific working groups within a Unit including its scope, authority, representation?**
    Yes. The charter isn’t limited in scope. A Security Working Group
    might need chartered exceptions from transparency and contribution
    rules. However, since the Units are self-managed, a top-down
    approach to defining the unit’s operational realm would be
    unfortunate. Instead, the Charter could mention specific topics
    within the unit’s responsibility domain, possibly with modifiers
    like “emphasizing” or “ensuring.” Then, it’s up to the unit to
    define its organization.

**17. What is a petition?**
    A petition is an instrument mentioned in the TYPO3 Association
    bylaws. The word means a formal written request or complaint
    requesting a particular action, commonly a change in policy or the
    correction of a wrong. For Units, petitions also provide a single
    point of contact through which requests can be made.

**18. What are examples of TYPO3 Association bodies that can petition Units?**
    Committees and units, but also the General Assembly, Board, or
    Business Control Committee, can formally petition a Unit. Even
    bodies that have decision-making power over Units may choose to
    petition them instead. In that case, the wish is clear, but the
    final decision is left up to the decision-making processes of the
    Unit.

**19. What happens if the Coordinator and Members do not allow new supporters to become a Member?**
    Though hopefully avoidable through unit size, it is a potential
    risk that the Unit decides to become closed to new members. Such a
    unit would be dysfunctional, requiring the TYPO3 Association Board
    to take action. If someone doesn’t feel welcomed into a unit, they
    should contact the Ombudspersons or the TYPO3 Association Board.

**20. How do we handle cross-concern initiatives, like today’s Stats Initiative?**
    This could be an initiative under the Feature Unit, but people
    from multiple units can participate in it. However, it would have
    to be founded in one Unit, with a coordinator from that unit.

**21. How can cross-cutting requirements, like accessibility, be applied in all Units, not just Stability & Compliance, where the accessibility topic belongs?**
    Accessibility is a compliance topic, but the Stability &
    Compliance and Feature units will have to work closely on this on
    a bilateral level. Certain types of required cooperation should go
    into their charters too. The Unit Cooperation Panel can also be
    involved, but under a more general inclusivity umbrella that
    includes the non-technical units too.
